CTF Practice: Spot the Phishing 🕵️‍♀️

Challenge: This page contains multiple CTF flags hidden in different ways. Find them by analyzing common phishing red flags.

From: BIM Maroc <bim-giveaway@promo-bim-win.net>
Subject: 🎉 BIG SALE + Free GAMING Laptop Giveaway!

Hi student 👋

Next Friday, BIM has 70% OFF and a FREE Gaming Laptop giveaway! You must confirm your account today to be eligible.

Click here now: http://bim-maroc-offre-gratuite.win/claim

To confirm identity, reply with your phone number + national ID.

🔎 CTF Hint: Some flags are in the page source. Others show up when you click.

🚩 Phishing Red Flags in this Email

  • Weird sender domain: promo-bim-win.net (not official)
  • Urgency pressure: “today” / “must confirm now”
  • Too-good-to-be-true: 70% OFF + free gaming laptop
  • Suspicious link: .win domain + http (not https)
  • Requests sensitive data: ID number, phone, etc.

✅ Safe Student Tips

  • Go directly to the official website (type it yourself).
  • Check the sender domain carefully.
  • Never share sensitive info by email.
  • When in doubt, ask a teacher/admin or report it.

Hint: In CTFs, flags are often hidden in HTML comments, meta tags, or via Inspect Element.
