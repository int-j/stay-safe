Stop. Learn. Go safe online.

Stay Safe is a three-week hybrid mission — on-site and online — that turns digital safety into a game. Clear each signal, unlock the next, and leave equipped to navigate the internet on your own terms.

// mission briefing

Why this mission exists

Four objectives guide every phase, every challenge, every session.

01 · AWARENESS

Recognize the threats

Spot phishing, scams, fake profiles, and manipulation before they spot you.

02 · NAVIGATION

Move through the web safely

Build habits for secure browsing, strong passwords, and protecting personal data.

03 · SOCIAL MEDIA

Post and share responsibly

Understand privacy settings, digital footprint, and what's worth putting online.

04 · ENGAGEMENT

Learn by playing

Challenges, points, and live sessions replace lectures — safety that sticks because it's fun.

// the journey

Three signals. Three weeks.

Every phase runs one week and follows the traffic light: stop and understand the danger, prepare with the right tools, then move forward with confidence.

RED_ALERT Stop & Recognize Week 1
+

The light is red because moving blindly online is dangerous. This week is about stopping, looking around, and naming the threats before doing anything else.

FOCUS

  • Cyber threats: phishing, scams, catfishing
  • Spotting manipulation and fake accounts
  • Understanding your digital footprint

ACTIVITIES

  • Live kickoff session with real case studies
  • "Spot the trap" challenge cards
  • Async quiz to unlock Orange Horizon
ORANGE_HORIZON Prepare & Equip Week 2
+

Orange means get ready. Now that the dangers are named, this week hands over the tools and habits to actually deal with them.

FOCUS

  • Passwords, 2FA, and account security
  • Privacy settings across social platforms
  • Safe communication and reporting tools

ACTIVITIES

  • Live workshop: secure your accounts, live
  • Hands-on lab with guided challenges
  • Team leaderboard checkpoint
GREEN_ZONE Go Forward Week 3
+

Green means go. Equipped with knowledge and tools, participants put it all together and move forward into the internet on their own terms.

FOCUS

  • Responsible social media use
  • Building a healthy online identity
  • Helping others stay safe too

ACTIVITIES

  • Final live session & capstone challenge
  • On-site closing ceremony
  • Certificates and badge reveal

// how it runs

Hybrid, gamified, weekly

SYNCHRONOUS

One live session per week

On-site and online participants join the same weekly session — real talks, real case studies, and a chance to ask questions live.

ASYNCHRONOUS

Challenges you unlock at your pace

Between sessions, complete bite-sized challenges and quizzes to earn points and unlock the next phase — no fixed schedule required.
